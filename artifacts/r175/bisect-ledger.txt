              CONCLUSION: Lume's non-default sdkconfig alone is NOT sufficient to reproduce.
              The failure requires Lume's application/component/binary context.

Next rounds: Lume-side group reverts (config x Lume-code interaction), temp copy of firmware,

STOP at 6 bisection rounds (budget <=8). Verdict: specific item locked at ASSOC stage
(CONFIG_ESP_COEX_SW_COEXIST_ENABLE, selected by BT_ENABLED=y): turning it off flips
assoc -> init (0x400) to assoc -> run (0x10). Full ALL PASS NOT reached: no DHCP/IP
after association; no LWIP/netif/DHCP config difference exists between stock and Lume,
so the residual is not config-driven. No firmware/sdkconfig.defaults change committed;
no fake PASS, no runner relaxation.
