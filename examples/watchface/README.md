# Watchface

Swipe left or right to cycle through digital, analog, information rings, and activity goals. `WATCHFACE_START_PAGE` selects the initial page (0–3), including for screenshots. Time comes from `new Date()`; without synchronization the device may show boot-relative time. The goals face draws three native AA progress arcs and three dim tracks (move kcal, exercise minutes, stand count), updated at 1 Hz. It runs a demo animation until IMU motion is detected; QMI8658 acceleration threshold and 300 ms debounce then produce a rough step estimate, not a calibrated pedometer. Its exercise and stand values are also illustrative estimates. Battery reads from `lume.pmic.battPercent()`; if unavailable the display labels its 78% fallback as DEMO. Hardware appearance, gesture feel, and activity accuracy need board acceptance.
